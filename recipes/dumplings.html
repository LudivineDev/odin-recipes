Pork & Cabbage Dumplings

45 min prep12 min cookMain · Dumplings

Photo: a2335825, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

A straightforward pork-and-cabbage dumpling, no ginger — homemade wrappers, a simple filling, and your choice of steaming or pan-searing. Makes about 40 dumplings.

Wrapper

  • 1 cup all-purpose or bread flour
  • ½ cup boiling water

Filling

  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 3 cups napa cabbage or bok choy, finely diced
  • 1 Tbsp garlic, minced
  • 1 stalk green onion, finely diced
  • ½ cup rehydrated shiitake mushrooms, diced (optional)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp white pepper
  • 1 tsp sesame oil

Dipping Sauce

  • 3 Tbsp Chinese black vinegar (or balsamic vinegar with 1 Tbsp water)

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour and boiling water into a dough and knead about 5 minutes, until smooth. Cover and rest 20–30 minutes.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the pork, cabbage, garlic, green onion, mushrooms (if using), salt, soy sauce, white pepper, and sesame oil. Mix well until evenly combined.
  3. Roll the dough into a log and cut into small pieces. Roll each piece into a thin circle, dusting with extra flour and keeping unrolled pieces covered so they don't dry out.
  4. Spoon a little less than a tablespoon of filling into the center of each wrapper. Dampen the edges with water, fold in half, and pleat the edge to seal.
  5. To steam: arrange in a steamer basket with space between dumplings and steam over boiling water, covered, for 10–12 minutes.
  6. To pan-sear: heat 2 Tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat and fry the dumplings until the bottoms are crisp. Pour in ¼ cup water, cover, and cook 5 minutes, then uncover and let the remaining water evaporate.
  7. Serve hot with the dipping sauce.

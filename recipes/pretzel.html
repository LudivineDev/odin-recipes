Soft Pretzels

15 min prep1 hr rest20 min bakeBread & Dough

Makes 12 pretzels. A baking-soda boil before the oven is what gives these their deep golden color and chewy, pretzel-shop crust.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups warm water, 110–115°F (480g)
  • 1½ Tbsp light brown sugar
  • 1 packet active dry yeast (8g)
  • 1½ tsp sea salt
  • 3 oz unsalted butter, melted (85g)
  • 5¼ cups all-purpose flour (750g)
  • 1 Tbsp oil, for the rising bowl
  • ¾ cup baking soda, for the boiling water
  • 1 egg yolk whisked with 1 Tbsp cold water, for egg wash
  • Coarse kosher or sea salt, for topping

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 450°F.
  2. In a stand mixer bowl, whisk together the water, brown sugar, and yeast. Let sit 5–7 minutes, until a foamy layer forms on top.
  3. Fit the dough hook, add the salt and melted butter, then mix in the flour a cup at a time. Knead on low-medium speed for 5 minutes, until smooth, soft, and not sticky.
  4. Oil a large bowl, add the dough, cover, and let rise until doubled in size, about 1 hour.
  5. Near the end of the rise, bring the baking soda and 1 gallon of water to a boil in a large pot.
  6. Roll a piece of dough into a 24-inch rope. Form a U shape, cross the two ends over each other, then press them onto the bottom of the U to form the pretzel shape. Repeat with the rest of the dough.
  7. Reduce the water to a simmer and cook each pretzel for 30 seconds, one or two at a time, then transfer to a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  8. Brush the tops with egg wash and sprinkle generously with coarse salt.
  9. Bake 12–15 minutes, until deep golden brown.

Tips

  • Reheat wrapped in foil at 350°F for 5–6 minutes, or in the microwave until hot.
  • Make ahead up to 1 day and reheat before serving; keeps wrapped in the fridge up to 4 days.
  • Shape into pretzel bites or rods instead, if you prefer.
  • If your mixer bowl runs cold, start with water closer to 118°F to compensate.
  • The dough should end up soft but not sticky — add a little extra flour while kneading if needed.

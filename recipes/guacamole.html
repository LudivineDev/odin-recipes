Guacamole

10 minDip · Appetizer

A quick, fresh guacamole made with just a handful of simple ingredients — no fancy technique required. Serve it as a dip with tortilla chips, or spoon it alongside tacos, burritos, and other Tex-Mex favorites.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe avocados
  • ¼ cup diced red onion
  • ¼ cup finely chopped fresh cilantro
  • Zest and juice of 2 limes
  • 1 small jalapeño pepper, diced
  • ½ tsp sea salt, plus more to taste
  • ½ tsp ground cumin (optional)
  • 1–2 Roma tomatoes, seeded and diced (optional)

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the avocado, onion, cilantro, lime zest and juice, jalapeño, salt, and cumin (if using). Mash with a fork until mixed but still a little chunky.
  2. Gently stir in the diced tomato, if using.
  3. Taste, adjust the salt, and serve right away.
